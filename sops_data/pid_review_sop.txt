STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PIPING & INSTRUMENTATION (P&ID) REVIEWS

1. OVERVIEW
This document outlines the standard process for reviewing and approving changes to Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs) within the refinery.

2. SECURITY CLEARANCE
All P&ID documents are highly classified. Review must happen on air-gapped workstations only. Do not upload drawings to external cloud services.

3. CALCULATION REQUIREMENTS
When a pipe diameter is modified, a pipe stress calculation must be performed. The maximum allowable stress for standard carbon steel piping in Sector 4 is 20,000 psi. If calculations exceed this, a secondary support structure is required.

4. APPROVAL WORKFLOW
Any changes affecting safety-critical systems must be documented in an "Approval Note". The note must include:
- The system affected.
- The calculated stress values.
- Justification for the change.
The generated Approval Note must be saved locally and physically signed by the Shift Supervisor.
